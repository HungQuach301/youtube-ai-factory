# AI Factory Charter

## Authority

`PRODUCTION_V7_GREENFIELD` is the controlling baseline. V5/V6 masters, materials, bindings, templates and rejected assets remain historical evidence and are never eligible inputs to V7 production.

## Product outcomes

1. A commercializable factory that can build and operate a YouTube channel end to end.
2. An owned English-language US channel in the niche **Hidden Systems Behind Money**.
3. A one-person operating model with Autopilot, Approval Gates and Manual modes using identical quality thresholds.

## Non-negotiable controls

- A plan authorizes work; only stored bytes or measurable artifacts prove completion.
- Only `FROZEN` artifacts are downstream-eligible.
- P0/P1 tolerance is zero at release gates.
- A stage receives at most three attempts and an attempt must improve by at least three points.
- Provider work uses request-level idempotency, terminal reconciliation and measured cost.
- A stopped chat is not evidence that an external request was cancelled.

# Multi-Channel Scale and Learning Architecture

**State:** `ACTIVE_NORMATIVE__IMPLEMENTATION_REQUIRED`  
**Policy:** `MULTI_CHANNEL_SCALE_AND_LEARNING_V1`  
**Effective:** 2026-08-24

## Scope and isolation

Factory capabilities are shared; channel identity, audience, economics, risk envelope, rights restrictions and learning remain versioned and isolated. A result from one channel is never silently promoted to another.

| Level | Owns | May inherit | Must not leak |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factory | Hard policies, provider/capability qualification, schemas, shared benchmarks and portfolio capacity | External standards and qualified provider bindings | One channel's credentials, private assets or identity |
| Channel | Audience, strategy, Visual/Voice DNA, locale, budget/risk and release policy | Factory-qualified capabilities | Another channel's performance conclusion or brand grammar |
| Series/Format | Repeatable narrative/treatment pattern and format benchmarks | Channel DNA and Factory techniques | Unqualified template behavior |
| Video/Experiment | Claims, blueprint, prediction, allocation and exact outcomes | Active parent versions | Learning from unrelated versions |

## Scale lifecycle

```text
PROPOSED -> SHADOW -> CANARY -> QUALIFIED -> SCALED
                 |        |          |
                 +-> REJECTED        +-> REVOKED -> ROLLED_BACK
```

Promotion requires minimum evidence across independent videos/time windows, stable inputs, no P0/P1 escape, acceptable first-pass yield, cost/latency within envelope and no unresolved rights/provider drift. One successful video cannot qualify a channel, format or capability.

## Learning record

Every production candidate stores the pre-publication prediction and hypothesis before outcomes are visible. Performance observations bind:

```text
channel/format/video version
claim_id and canonical timebase
shot/treatment/capability/provider versions
exact published artifact and publication context
impressions, CTR, retention/timecode and audience signals
cost, latency, first-pass yield and remediation
measurement window, sample size and known confounders
```

Raw observation is immutable. A `LearningPromotion` creates a new policy/DNA/format/capability version; it never edits the historical decision.

## Experiment validity

- Define hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, comparison unit and stopping rule before publication.
- Avoid overlapping material changes when attribution matters.
- Compare equivalent audience, distribution and measurement windows; label non-randomized conclusions as observational.
- Do not promote from a single video, underpowered sample, bot/anomaly period or unresolved distribution shift.
- Retention findings map to exact timecodes and treatments; aggregate channel averages cannot prove a shot-level cause.
- Novel format, audience, language, provider/model or policy version restarts the relevant qualification scope.

## Portfolio arbitration

Channels compete through comparable expected value, confidence, accepted-output cost, capacity need, risk and strategic option value. Shared workers/providers use policy-bound quotas and reservations so one channel cannot starve another or spend its budget. Safety, rights, incident containment and exact-master completion take precedence over new experiments.

## Champion/challenger and rollback

The active champion remains in force until a challenger passes qualification and promotion evidence. Rollback selects a prior immutable version for future work and records reason, affected scope and recovery test. A channel-level incident may pause only the affected channel unless evidence shows a shared Factory capability or provider is compromised; shared dependency failure triggers bounded Factory-wide containment.

## Minimum learning authority

The Standard Registry owns quantitative floors by decision class. Until calibrated floors exist, learning is `ADVISORY` and cannot autonomously change hard gates, release/publication policy, rights rules, factual thresholds or channel identity. AI may recommend; only the typed promotion authority can activate a new version.

## Current boundary

Hidden Systems and R22 are the first canary scope. No multi-channel scaling authority is created by this document; implementation, controlled evidence and explicit promotion remain required.
